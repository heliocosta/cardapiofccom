Following live football in the United Kingdom means navigating one of the most fragmented broadcast markets in the sport. Between Premier League packages, EFL coverage, FA Cup rounds, and midweek European nights, matches are spread across free-to-air public channels, pay-TV networks, and standalone streaming platforms.

Here is how UK football rights fit together—and how to verify the exact **official UK channel** before kick-off.

## How Live Football Rights Are Split in the UK

Unlike territories where a single broadcaster owns an entire league, UK rights are sold in distinct competition and kick-off packages:

- **Sky Sports (and NOW Sports)**: The primary UK home for the Premier League alongside full EFL Championship, League One, League Two, Carabao Cup, Scottish Premiership, and Women's Super League coverage.
- **TNT Sports (and discovery+)**: Carries live Premier League fixtures (typically early Saturday kick-offs and selected midweek rounds), the UEFA Champions League, UEFA Europa League, UEFA Conference League, and FA Cup ties.
- **BBC Sport (BBC One & BBC iPlayer)**: Free-to-air UK coverage (with a valid TV Licence) for selected FA Cup matches, Women's Super League games, Scottish Cup ties, and major international tournaments.
- **ITV (ITV1, ITV4 & ITVX)**: Free-to-air coverage for England men's and women's qualifiers and friendlies, FA Cup selections, EFL Cup ties, and major international finals.
- **Amazon Prime Video**: Streams top-pick Tuesday night UEFA Champions League fixtures in the UK as part of standard Amazon Prime membership.
- **DAZN**: Carries selected women's football competitions and international club events in the UK market.

## Understanding the Saturday 3pm Blackout

If a Premier League or EFL match kicks off at 15:00 on a Saturday and does not appear on any UK television schedule, it is not an oversight. Under the UK's Saturday afternoon blackout (14:45–17:15), broadcasters are prohibited from airing live football on UK screens during that window, while highlights follow later in the evening on BBC's *Match of the Day* or official club channels.
