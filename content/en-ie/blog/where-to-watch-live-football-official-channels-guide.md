Live football broadcasting in the Republic of Ireland has its own distinct rights landscape. While Irish viewers share several pay-TV networks with the UK, Ireland also benefits from dedicated free-to-air European club coverage and exclusive Saturday 3pm Premier League broadcasts that are not aired next door.

Knowing which Irish broadcaster holds each competition makes matchday planning effortless.

## Official Football Broadcasters in Ireland

Across domestic, British, and European competitions, licensed football coverage in Ireland comes from five core broadcasters:

- **RTÉ2 & RTÉ Player**: Ireland's free-to-air public service broadcaster shows Republic of Ireland men's and women's internationals, live League of Ireland fixtures, selected Tuesday UEFA Champions League games, and major FIFA/UEFA tournaments.
- **Virgin Media Two & Virgin Media Play**: Free-to-air commercial television carrying live UEFA Champions League, UEFA Europa League, UEFA Conference League, and selected international matches in Ireland.
- **Sky Sports (and NOW Sports)**: Broadcasts live Premier League fixtures, all EFL divisions, Carabao Cup ties, the Scottish Premiership, and the Women's Super League across Sky, Virgin Media, and NOW.
- **TNT Sports (Sports Extra & discovery+)**: Shows live Premier League matches alongside comprehensive UEFA Champions League, Europa League, and Conference League coverage.
- **Premier Sports 1 ROI**: Holds exclusive rights in the Republic of Ireland to broadcast a **live Saturday 3pm Premier League match**, as well as FA Cup fixtures, Scottish football, and Spanish LaLiga ties.

## Why Ireland's Schedule Differs from the UK

Because the Republic of Ireland is a separate broadcast territory outside the UK's Saturday 3pm blackout rule, Irish supporters can legally watch a live Saturday afternoon Premier League kick-off on Premier Sports 1 ROI. In addition, both RTÉ2 and Virgin Media Two carry free-to-air European club fixtures midweek, giving fans in Ireland strong free-to-air options alongside pay-TV packs.
