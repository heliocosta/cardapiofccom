## What we collect

CardápioFC does not require an account and does not run any advertising. With your consent we measure visits using Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are useful. We also may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies.

## Legal basis and retention

Analytics cookies are used only with your consent (consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR where it applies). Your choice is stored on your device for 180 days and then asked again. You can withdraw consent at any time.

## Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, and object to processing. You may also complain to the supervisory authority listed below.
