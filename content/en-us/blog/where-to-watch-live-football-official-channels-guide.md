Watching live soccer in the United States offers more match coverage than ever before—nearly every top domestic and European league is available live every weekend. The trade-off is that US broadcast rights are spread across several competing media groups, each pairing linear television networks with a dedicated streaming service.

Knowing which broadcaster holds each tournament helps you avoid switching apps at kickoff.

## Major Official Soccer Broadcasters in the USA

Across club and international competitions, official US soccer rights are divided among five major media homes:

- **NBC Sports, USA Network & Peacock**: The official English-language US home of the **Premier League**, splitting weekend fixtures between NBC over-the-air broadcasts, USA Network on cable, and live streaming on Peacock (with Spanish-language coverage across Telemundo and Universo).
- **CBS Sports, CBS Sports Network & Paramount+**: Carries every **UEFA Champions League**, **UEFA Europa League**, and **UEFA Conference League** match live on Paramount+, alongside Italian **Serie A** and selected **NWSL** coverage.
- **ESPN, ABC & ESPN+**: Holds US rights to **LaLiga**, the **Bundesliga**, England's **FA Cup**, Copa del Rey, DFB-Pokal, and US Open Cup, streaming hundreds of fixtures live on ESPN+.
- **FOX, FS1 & Telemundo**: Broadcasts major international tournaments including **FIFA World Cup** and **UEFA European Championship / Copa América** packages, alongside selected Liga MX and CONCACAF matches.
- **Apple TV (MLS Season Pass)**: Streams every **Major League Soccer (MLS)** and **Leagues Cup** match globally without local blackouts, with select matches also simulcast on FOX or FS1.

## How to Plan a US Soccer Matchday

Because European leagues kick off early in the morning across US time zones while MLS and Liga MX run into the evening, checking a single verified schedule saves time:

1. **Verify kickoff times and networks**: Browse [today's US soccer matches](/en-us/matches/) for kickoffs in US Eastern time and confirmed English or Spanish broadcast listings.
2. **Track each league separately**: Since Champions League, Premier League, LaLiga, and MLS sit on different platforms, check [soccer leagues and cups](/en-us/leagues/) before the weekend begins.
3. **Confirm TV vs. streaming-only fixtures**: Many marquee matches air on NBC, CBS, ABC, or FOX while simultaneous fixtures stream exclusively on Peacock, Paramount+, or ESPN+. Explore the [US broadcasters directory](/en-us/channels/) for official channel details.
