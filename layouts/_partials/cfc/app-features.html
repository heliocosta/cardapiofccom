Native Mobile App Features

Everything Football Fans Need, in Your Pocket

The CardápioFC mobile app is loaded with live match companion features you won't find anywhere else. Completely free, no ads, no piracy.

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "tv" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Instant "Where to Watch" Guide

Know immediately which TV channel, cable network, or legal streaming service broadcasts any football match in your country.

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "clock" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Real-Time Scores & Match Clock

Follow live scores, elapsed minutes, halftime/fulltime results, and penalty shootouts as they happen in real time.

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "stream" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} One-Tap Launch to Streaming Apps

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "calendar" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Daily Football "Menu" by Date

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "bell" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Custom Reminders & Alerts

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "star" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Personalised Favourites & Watchlist

Follow your favourite clubs, leagues, and channels, and bookmark upcoming games in "My Agenda" so you never lose track.

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "check" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Visual Pitch Lineups & Formations

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "check" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Interactive Lineup Builder

{{ partial "cfc/icon.html" (dict "name" "globe" "class" "w-5 h-5") }} Multi-Country & Big-Screen Companion

Check live schedules on your wrist with Wear OS, browse on Android TV, or switch countries when traveling abroad.
